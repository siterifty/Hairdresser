About Tiffaney's Crown & Curls

Tiffaney's Crown & Curls Hair Studio specializes in short hair and the classic styles that never go out of style — clean, neat, and beautiful, every single time. With over 25 years of experience behind the chair, Tiffaney brings a level of craft and care that only comes from decades of doing this work.

From pixie cuts and finger waves to Marcel curls, roller sets, and edge styling, every appointment is about more than hair. As the studio's motto goes: it's not just hair, it's confidence.

Let Tiffaney bring out the best in your short hair.
